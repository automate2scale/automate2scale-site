Before you start

Questions founders ask first.

What does it cost?
The Strategy Session is complimentary. From there, the Blueprint Session is $497 (credited toward the System Install if you proceed within 30 days), the 90-Day Roadmap Session is $1,997, and the System Install runs $4,997 a month in 90-day sprints. If you only need the CRM working now, the CRM Track starts at $297 a month. See every stage.
How much of my time will this take?
Less than you are spending today chasing updates. You and your leadership team join the working sessions and the weekly meeting rhythm; we do the design, the build and the automation. The point of the system is to take work off your plate, not add to it.
What if we are not a fit?
Then we will tell you on the first call. You still leave the Strategy Session with a Constraint Confirmation Brief and a documented next step, so the 30 minutes pays for itself either way.
Why not hire an EOS Implementer and a CRM agency separately?
You can. The risk is that strategy and technology stay separate: one team clarifies priorities, another builds tools, and your company is left to connect the two. We integrate operating rhythm, revenue process, CRM, automation, reporting and AI into one installed system.
Are you replacing EOS?
No. We install the operating discipline founder-led companies need, including priorities, ownership, scorecards and meeting rhythm. The difference is that we connect that discipline directly to revenue operations, automation and AI-enabled execution.
Is this mainly a CRM implementation?
No. The CRM is part of the system. The real work is designing how leads are captured, followed up, sold, measured and improved. The platform supports the operating model. It is not the strategy.
Is this an AI consulting offer?
No. AI is the execution layer. We use it to add capacity, reduce manual work and support better decisions, on a foundation of strategy, ownership, process and measurement.
Where should we start?
Start with the free A³ Readiness Scorecard if you want a quick view of the constraint. Book a Strategy Session if you already know the business needs a more serious operating system.
Who is this not for?
Companies looking for a cheap software setup, a disconnected automation project or a pile of AI tools. It is for founder-led companies ready to install a better way to run revenue and execution.
