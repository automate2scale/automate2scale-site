Less than you are spending today chasing updates. You and your leadership team join the working sessions and the weekly meeting rhythm; we do the design, the build and the automation. The point of the system is to take work off your plate, not add to it.
What if we are not a fit?
Then we will tell you on the first call. You still leave the Strategy Session with a Constraint Confirmation Brief and a documented next step, so the 30 minutes pays for itself either way.
Why not hire an EOS Implementer and a CRM agency separately?
You can. The risk is that strategy and technology stay separate: one team clarifies priorities, another builds tools, and your company is left to connect the two. We integrate operating rhythm, revenue process, CRM, automation, reporting and AI into one installed system.
Are you replacing EOS?
No. We install the operating discipline owner-led companies need, including priorities, ownership, scorecards and meeting rhythm. The difference is that we connect that discipline directly to revenue operations, automation and AI-enabled execution.
Is this mainly a CRM implementation?
No. The CRM is part of the system. The real work is designing how leads are captured, followed up, sold, measured and improved. The platform supports the operating model. It is not the strategy.
Is this an AI consulting offer?
No. AI is the execution layer. We use it to add capacity, reduce manual work and support better decisions, on a foundation of strategy, ownership, process and measurement.

Why an operating system is required to scale

Hustle built the company. It cannot scale it.

Early on, the founder is the operating system. Every decision, follow-up and priority runs through one person, and that works until the company outgrows one person's bandwidth.

After that point, adding people adds complexity, not capacity. Without a shared vision, clear seats, a weekly rhythm and one set of numbers, every new hire is another person waiting on you. An operating system replaces founder memory with a structure the whole team runs.

"Nothing moves unless I push it."

The founder is the bottleneck

Decisions, approvals and problems queue up behind one person, so growth stops when you do.

"We set goals. Then the quarter happens."

Priorities drift

Big ideas pile up, everything feels urgent and the few things that matter most never get finished.

"I'm not sure who owns that."

Unclear seats

Good people overlap, gaps go unowned and accountability turns into a conversation about personalities.

"We keep solving the same problems."

Meetings without resolution

Issues get discussed every week and fixed never, because there is no discipline for solving them at the root.

Questions

What leaders usually ask.

Do we have to run EOS® to work with you?
No. If you already run EOS, we strengthen it and connect it to your revenue system. If you don't, we install the same proven disciplines (vision, seats, Rocks, Scorecard and a weekly meeting rhythm) in a way that fits your company.
We already work with an EOS Implementer. Is this different?
Yes, and the two work well together. An Implementer teaches your leadership team the system. An Integrator runs it every day inside the business. Mark fills that seat on a fractional basis or develops the leader who will, and then connects the rhythm to your CRM, automation and AI.
Do we need a full-time Integrator?
Not always. Many owner-led companies in our range ($1M–$20M) start with a fractional Integrator to build the habits, then promote or hire into the seat once the role is clearly defined and the rhythm is working.
